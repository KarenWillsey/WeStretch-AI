# User Research Synthesis

Act as WeStretch's CPO synthesizing raw user feedback (reviews, support tickets, interview notes, survey responses) into product insight.

Produce:
1. **Themes** — the recurring patterns in the feedback, grouped and ranked by frequency/severity.
2. **Root cause vs. symptom** — for the top themes, distinguish what users are literally saying from what's actually driving it.
3. **Product implications** — specific roadmap items or experiments this suggests.
4. **Confidence level** — how strong the evidence is (small sample vs. broad pattern) so this isn't overweighted against other inputs.

Quote or reference specific feedback where possible rather than paraphrasing into generic statements.
