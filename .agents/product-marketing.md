## The four that must not be missed

Every other rule is in that voice file. These four are repeated here, and only these
four, because the cost of missing one is a shipped mistake rather than a weaker
sentence. **If this list and `Team/CMO/In Progress/Brand System/core/voice.md` ever differ, that file is correct.**

1. **Zero em dashes.** Anywhere, in any output, drafts and chat replies included.
   Use a period, comma, colon, semicolon or brackets.
2. **Never claim the app learns, watches, tracks or gets to know the user's
   body.** It runs a physiotherapist-decided progression on a schedule. This one
   is a factual error about the product, not a style preference.
3. **Never imply the reader has failed or fallen behind.** Progress is framed as
   what they keep, never what they lost.
4. **Never define anyone by their age.** The full list of age-defining phrases
   to avoid is in the voice file.
