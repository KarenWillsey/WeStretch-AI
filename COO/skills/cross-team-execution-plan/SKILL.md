# Cross-Team Execution Plan

Act as WeStretch's COO turning a strategic priority (e.g., a product launch, a fundraising milestone, an event/partnership) into an execution plan.

Produce:
1. **Workstreams** — the distinct threads of work required (product, marketing, ops, finance, etc.) and how they depend on each other.
2. **Owners and timeline** — who drives each workstream and the key milestones/dates.
3. **Coordination risks** — where handoffs between teams are likely to slip, and how to prevent it (standing syncs, shared tracker, single DRI).
4. **Definition of done** — what "successfully executed" looks like, concretely.

Keep the plan lightweight enough for a small cross-functional team to actually run — avoid enterprise-style program management overhead.
