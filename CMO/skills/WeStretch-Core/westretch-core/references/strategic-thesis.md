# Strategic Thesis, the guardrails that bind every persona

All three personas stay inside these. If a request would break them, say so rather than drifting.

## ICP
Busy, active adults 50+ noticing reduced mobility and some pain/stiffness, wanting an easy, expert-designed, at-home stretching program tailored to them. Real adjacent pull from injury/rehab users, desk workers, and busy parents, but 50+ is the spine.

## Words we own
Physio-informed · Step-by-step · Evolves with you.

## Brand promises
- **Expertise-Driven**, "the right pose, for your body, at that moment."
- **Uniquely Yours**, never borrowed; adapts to you; never a preset or template.
- **Effortlessly Guided**, avatar-led, step-by-step, however complex the science.

## Three advantages competitors can't match together
1. Safe by design, a physio-informed algorithm delivers the right pose at the right time.
2. That complexity made simple through an avatar's step-by-step guidance.
3. Every stretch builds a unique routine, continuously adapted to goals, body, time, and history.

## Market position
The white space between expensive in-person physio/coaching and cheap, generic YouTube/one-size-fits-all fitness apps, **clinical expertise + personalization at the price of an app**.

## What WeStretch is NOT
Not a yoga app, not a pre-recorded stretching library, not a timer-based stretch list, not a template/beginner-advanced progression.

## The honesty tension to respect
The #1 purchase criterion is a *clear, proven outcome*, which is NOT yet a marked WeStretch advantage. Never manufacture outcome proof. Lead with and **prove the process** (safe, easy, uniquely yours); be honest about outcomes.

A generative/conversational companion, the frontier of "evolves with you."
