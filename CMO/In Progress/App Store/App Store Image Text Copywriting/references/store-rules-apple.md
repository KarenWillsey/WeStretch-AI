# Apple App Store, rules the copy must obey

Verified from Apple developer documentation. (Google Play differs and will be added when needed, for now this skill produces Apple only.)

## Slots
- Up to **10 screenshots** per device size + language. Format **PNG or JPEG only**, a literal GIF cannot be a screenshot.
- Up to **3 app preview videos**, which appear **first**, before screenshots. A portrait video takes the first slot on the product page.
- So "1 video + up to 9 stills" is the working model, with headroom.

## The video (Part 1), the critical rule
- The app-preview video must be **real, on-screen footage captured from within the app**. Apple rejects promo/marketing-style content, "stylized animations," and title-card slideshows.
- Deliver the hook headlines as **caption overlays on real app footage** (Ada guiding a routine, the body-part selector, the step-by-step flow). Always tell the design team this.
- ~15–30 seconds, under 500 MB.

## Text / claims (applies to Part 1 captions and Part 2 titles)
- No features, content, or pricing shown that isn't actually in the app (misleading = rejection).
- No unprovable superlatives ("best," "#1").
- No absolute safety claims ("every pose is safe"), use honest safety-by-design language ("works around your injuries and problem areas").
- No fabricated testimonials, ratings, or outcome claims on the screens.
- Keep competitor names out of visible text (ASO keywords/ads only).

## Where real proof belongs
Real testimonials, streak numbers, and before/afters that stores restrict in screenshots belong on the **website and in emails**, where these rules don't apply. Drive store visitors there rather than crowding the screens with claims.
