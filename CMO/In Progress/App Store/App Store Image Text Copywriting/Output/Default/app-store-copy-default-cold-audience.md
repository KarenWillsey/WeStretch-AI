Goal: the default App Store product page. Audience: cold browsers who have never heard of WeStretch, arriving from search or browse with no prior trust. Produced with the app-store-copy skill running on westretch-core (Chase, Expert, Marg, Strategic Thesis, honesty guardrail, grading loop).

## Chase strategy (shown, not hidden)

- **Six-Axis read of a cold default-page visitor:** low suggestibility, low connection, low expectancy, no compliance earned yet. Focus is moderate but fragile: they searched something like "stretching app" and are comparing several results in seconds. The first job is to raise openness and expectancy through recognition, not to ask for anything. No ask appears until the final card.
- **FATE levers:** **Tribe** and **Emotion** lead. The default page must instantly say "this was made for a body like mine, not for 20-year-olds," which is the single strongest belonging signal for the ICP (active adults 50+, plus injury, desk, and parent adjacents). Emotion is recognition and hope, never fear of decline. **Authority** supports in the middle of the sequence: real, earned only (licensed physiotherapists, the mechanism in plain words). Focus is handled by the opener naming a felt experience.
- **Identity built:** "I'm someone who takes care of how I move." The page invites them into that identity; it never shames them for not having it.
- **Why this angle fits:** a cold visitor cannot yet believe outcome claims, and the Strategic Thesis forbids manufacturing them. So the page proves the process (safe by design, guided, uniquely yours) and closes with **self-generated proof**: a dare to give it honest daily time and notice the change themselves. Marg trusts a dare to test far more than a promise to feel great.

## Appendix

- **Where the honesty guardrail blocked a juiced grade:** Expert proposed "Feel looser in two weeks" as the screen 9 title (would likely have graded A on Attention and Act). Blocked: it promises a felt outcome we cannot prove. The dare framing keeps the same energy without the claim. Also blocked: any stretch-library count on screen, since the true current count has been stated inconsistently across surfaces; confirm the one true number before ever publishing it.
- **Real proof that belongs on web or email instead of the store:** real testimonials, streak and usage numbers, before/after stories, and the founder story (Karen Willsey, programmer and mother of six who built the app because nothing fit real bodies). Apple restricts or rejects most of these in screenshots; they convert better on the website and in onboarding email anyway.
- **Optional personalization notes:** none for the default page; it must work with zero data about the visitor. CPP variants (pain relief, seniors, free) should re-run this skill per theme rather than editing these lines in place.
- **Compliance check (store-rules-apple.md):** no superlatives, no pricing on screen, no absolute safety claims, no competitor names in visible text, no fabricated ratings or testimonials, and every feature shown (body-area selection, Ada guidance, time selection, adaptive routines) exists in the app.
