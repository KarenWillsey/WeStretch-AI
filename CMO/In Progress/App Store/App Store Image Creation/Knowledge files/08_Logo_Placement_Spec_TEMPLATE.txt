ASSET RULE
Use the original logo knowledge file exactly as supplied.
Never regenerate, redraw, recolour, crop, stretch, compress, retype, or otherwise modify the logo.
Preserve transparency and original aspect ratio.

PLACEMENT REFERENCE
Measurements are based on the visible logo artwork in the approved 853 × 1844 reference.

Visible logo top edge: 4.93% of canvas height
Visible logo horizontal centre: 49.53% of canvas width
Target visible logo height: 2.39% of canvas height
Target visible logo width on the approved phone aspect ratio: approximately 61.08% of canvas width

RESPONSIVE SIZE RULE
Scale the logo by height, not by width:

1. Set the visible logo artwork height to 2.39% of canvas height.
2. Preserve the logo’s original aspect ratio.
3. Centre it at 49.53% of canvas width.
4. Place its visible top edge at 4.93% of canvas height.
5. Never allow its visible width to exceed 61.08% of canvas width.

This height-based rule keeps the logo from becoming oversized on the wider 13-inch output while matching the approved phone design.

CLEAR SPACE
Keep the area around the logo free of title text, subtitle text, photographs, highlights, and decorative elements.
In the approved design, the visible logo bottom is approximately 7.32% of canvas height.
The visible title begins at approximately 11.93% of canvas height.
Preserve this visual separation.

QUALITY CHECK
The logo must be identical in every variation.
The logo must occupy the same position in matched outputs.
Check the visible artwork bounds, not merely the transparent PNG canvas bounds.
Do not use a generated approximation of the logo.
