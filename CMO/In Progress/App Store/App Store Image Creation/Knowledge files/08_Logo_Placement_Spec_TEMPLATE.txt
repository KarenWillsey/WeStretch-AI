Reference canvas: 853 × 1844 px (approved pickleball phone design).
All percentages are derived from this reference.

Measure from visible logo artwork — NOT from transparent PNG bounds.

Visible logo top edge:          4.93% of canvas height
Visible logo horizontal centre: 49.53% of canvas width
Target visible logo height:     2.39% of canvas height
Maximum visible logo width:     61.08% of canvas width

RESPONSIVE SCALING RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Scale by height, not by width. This prevents the logo from becoming
oversized on wider outputs (e.g. 13-inch canvas) while matching the
approved phone design at all sizes.

Steps:
1. Detect the non-transparent visible artwork bounds of the logo PNG.
2. Scale uniformly until visible artwork height = 2.39% of canvas height.
3. Preserve aspect ratio throughout.
4. Centre the visible artwork at 49.53% of canvas width.
5. Position the visible artwork top edge at 4.93% of canvas height.
6. Confirm visible width does not exceed 61.08% of canvas width.

Never estimate placement visually. Always calculate from canvas dimensions.
