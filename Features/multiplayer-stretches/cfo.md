## Alignment check

Spend is proportionate to the feature's current status as an **unvalidated hypothesis**, not a committed growth bet — nearly all cost is internal labor, with marketing and infra spend deliberately deferred until the CGO experiment (see [cgo.md](cgo.md)) produces a retention read. This matches the "cheap to test, expensive to scale" pattern the CPO's roadmap doc recommends.

## Variance flags

- Watch engineering time against the 3-5 week estimate — the friend-graph dependency flagged in [cto.md](cto.md) is the biggest risk to this holding; if WeStretch has no existing contact/friend model, add 2-3 weeks and re-approve before proceeding.
- No paid marketing spend should occur before the 30-day retention checkpoint — treat any request to spend earlier as a variance requiring explicit re-approval.

## Recommendation

Approve the async MVP at estimated cost as a **retention-validation experiment**, not a full feature launch. Hold budget for live sync and paid marketing until the 30-day checkpoint in [coo.md](coo.md) produces a result — do not pre-commit runway to the more expensive path before it's earned.
