# CMO — Campaign Plan: Path to 1,000 DAU (Bootstrap)

_Skills applied: `CMO/skills/campaign-planning`, `CMO/skills/brand-messaging-review`_

## Campaign plan

### Objective and audience

Objective: drive organic installs and reactivations sufficient to support the DAU target, with **$0 assumed cash marketing spend** per the stated bootstrap constraint. Audience: fitness-motivated people already in the habit-formation window (new year's resolution audience does not apply — current planning date is outside that seasonal window, flagged so no one assumes a seasonal tailwind that isn't there).

### Channel plan

No paid channel is proposed — with zero budget, "budget allocation" below is **labor-hours**, not dollars:

1. **App Store / Play Store optimization (ASO)** — keywords, screenshots, preview video. Highest leverage-per-hour of anything on this list; zero cash cost.
2. **Organic short-form content** (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) tied to real product moments (streaks, first-workout wins) — feeds the same share moment [cgo.md](cgo.md) is instrumenting.
3. **Barter partnerships** — local gyms/studios, micro-fitness creators: free premium access or swag in exchange for a post or a QR-code flyer, not cash.
4. **Referral amplification** — CMO's job here is packaging/messaging for the invite flow [cgo.md](cgo.md) and [cto.md](cto.md) are building, not building a separate channel.

### Creative angle

Lead with consistency and progress, not intensity — matches what's plausible for a stretching/mobility-adjacent fitness product. **Caveat**: no documented WeStretch brand voice/positioning exists in this repo. This creative direction is an assumption, flagged per the brand review below, not a confirmed brand guideline.

### Success metrics

Since there is no paid spend, there is no $CAC to target. Track **organic installs by source, weekly**, and treat "labor-hours per install" as the internal efficiency metric instead. Kill or double down per channel every 2 weeks based on that read, not on a fixed campaign end date.

## Brand messaging review

### Voice check

Cannot be performed with confidence — **no brand voice/style guide is documented anywhere in this repo.** Flagging this explicitly rather than inventing a tone and checking against it.

### Positioning check

Same gap: no documented differentiation statement exists to check new copy against. The "consistency/progress" angle above is CMO's working assumption, not a verified position.

### Clarity

Any campaign copy should be tested against one bar regardless of brand-guide status: would someone with no fitness background understand the value in one sentence? Recommend a lightweight 5-user comprehension check on ASO copy before shipping it wide — cheap, and doesn't require the missing brand doc.

### Revisions / recommendation

Before this campaign plan is fully trustworthy, produce a one-page brand/positioning doc (owner: CMO, reviewer: CEO). This is a fast, cheap fix — do it in week 1, in parallel with everything else, not as a blocker to starting ASO or content work.
