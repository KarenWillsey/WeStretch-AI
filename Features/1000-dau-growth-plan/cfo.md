# CFO — Budget & Financial Model: Path to 1,000 DAU

_Skills applied: `Team/CFO/skills/budget-planning`, `Team/CFO/skills/financial-model-review`_

## Budget planning

### Budget breakdown

Per the stated bootstrap constraint: **$0 new cash marketing spend** for this initiative. The real cost is opportunity cost of the existing team's time across [cto.md](cto.md)'s build items, [cmo.md](cmo.md)'s content/ASO work, and [cgo.md](cgo.md)'s experiment design/analysis. No historical spend figures exist in this repo to compare against, so this is a from-scratch budget, not a variance review.

### Alignment check

Spend (all labor, no cash) is concentrated on instrumentation, the referral loop, and organic content — consistent with a zero-cash-budget growth strategy. If any role doc above quietly assumed a marketing spend line, that would be a misalignment; none currently do.

### Variance flags

Not applicable — no prior-period actuals exist to compare against.

### Recommendation

Hold cash spend at zero through the first 4–6 weeks, gated explicitly on [cgo.md](cgo.md)'s K-factor read. Spending on paid acquisition before that read exists would mean buying installs at an unknown CAC to hit an arbitrary deadline — poor discipline for a bootstrap-stage company regardless of how the deadline pressure feels. If K-factor comes back below [cgo.md](cgo.md)'s 0.15 threshold, that is the trigger point to bring a small, capped paid-spend test or a timeline extension to the CEO/board as an explicit decision — not to quietly keep pushing organic tactics past the point they're working.

## Financial model review

### Key metrics

Burn rate, runway, CAC, and LTV are all **unconfirmed** in this repo — flagging clearly rather than presenting estimates as fact, per this skill's explicit instruction.

### Sensitivity

This plan's biggest financial sensitivity isn't a modeling assumption, it's the K-factor result in [cgo.md](cgo.md): a small change in that number is the difference between "1,000 DAU is achievable at $0 spend" and "it isn't, and a real budget or timeline conversation is needed."

### Scenario read

- **Best case**: K-factor clears threshold early, referral loop compounds, 1,000 DAU reached near the 12-week mark at ~$0 incremental cash cost.
- **Base case**: K-factor is marginal; DAU grows meaningfully but likely lands short of 1,000 by week 12 without added spend — decision point moves to week 6, per above.
- **Worst case**: activation/retention fixes underperform and the loop never gets a clean read because instrumentation ([cio.md](cio.md)) lags — in this case the failure is diagnostic, not strategic, and the fix is closing the measurement gap, not abandoning the approach.

### Recommendation

Don't commit to hitting 1,000 DAU in exactly 3 months as a hard promise to the board/CEO — commit to the phased plan in [coo.md](coo.md) and to an honest go/no-go read at the week-6 mark. Protecting the decision point matters more here than protecting the deadline.
