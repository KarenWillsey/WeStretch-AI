# CHRO — Org & Hiring: Path to 1,000 DAU

_Skills applied: `Team/CHRO/skills/hiring-plan`, `Team/CHRO/skills/org-culture-review`_

## Hiring plan

### Role justification

**None justified for this window.** The stated constraint is bootstrap — no dedicated marketing budget, small team. A 3-month, aggressive DAU push is exactly the kind of pressure that generates hiring requests that don't map to a durable need; pushing back on that here, consistent with this skill's mandate.

### Role definition / sequencing

Not applicable this quarter. If the K-factor experiment in [cgo.md](cgo.md) succeeds and the company wants to sustain growth beyond this window, that's the point to revisit a growth-marketing or lifecycle-engineering hire — not before there's evidence the loop works.

### Sourcing/budget considerations

If truly bounded, one-off contractor help (a few hours of short-form video editing, or ASO screenshot design) is reasonable and cheap; this is scoped task work, not headcount, and should be budgeted through [cfo.md](cfo.md) as a small one-time cost, not a hiring decision.

## Org & culture review

### Structure check

With 10 executive roles nominally in play but a bootstrap team, several of these roles are almost certainly the same 1–3 people wearing multiple hats. That's fine operationally, but **decision rights still need to be explicit per workstream** — e.g., who actually decides the K-factor threshold isn't being met and the plan needs to change ([cgo.md](cgo.md) proposes it, but escalation per [cfo.md](cfo.md) means someone has to own that call). Don't let "everyone is everything" blur into "no one owns the call."

### Retention risk

A hard 12-week push toward a 5x+ DAU target, on a small team, with no new hires and no budget, is a real burnout setup — this is worth naming directly rather than treating as an unavoidable cost of ambition. Watch the people carrying [cto.md](cto.md)'s build items and [cmo.md](cmo.md)'s content cadence simultaneously; that's the likely overload point given the roadmap in [coo.md](coo.md).

### Culture signal

Recommend the weekly growth review [coo.md](coo.md) proposes double as a check-in on workload, not purely a metrics readout — a standing sync that only ever asks "did the number move" reinforces exactly the pressure this section flags as a risk.

### Recommendations

- No hiring this quarter.
- One lightweight weekly pulse check on team capacity, folded into the existing growth-review sync rather than a separate meeting.
- If the K-factor experiment misses its threshold and leadership decides to inject a budget ([cfo.md](cfo.md)), revisit whether that budget should include a small contractor allocation before considering a full hire.
