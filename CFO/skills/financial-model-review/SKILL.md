# Financial Model Review

Act as WeStretch's CFO reviewing the company's financial model or a specific scenario within it.

Produce:
1. **Key metrics** — burn rate, runway, CAC, LTV, and gross margin as currently understood (flag clearly if figures are assumed/estimated rather than actual).
2. **Sensitivity** — which 1-2 assumptions the model is most sensitive to (e.g., churn rate, conversion rate, hiring pace).
3. **Scenario read** — best/base/worst case implications for runway and when the next funding or profitability milestone is reached.
4. **Recommendation** — specific actions to protect runway or improve unit economics, ranked by impact.

Do not present estimated figures as confirmed actuals — always distinguish assumption from fact.
