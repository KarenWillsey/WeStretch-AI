# Meta Ads: Women 50-65, 5 Angles, 25 Concepts

## Decision

25 static-ad concepts for Meta (Facebook/Instagram), organized into 5
angles of 5 ads each, mapped to the awareness ladder from unaware pain to
ready-to-buy offer. Built for testing, not for all 25 to run at once: pick
2-3 per angle for a first wave, per the skill's testing discipline (one
variable changed at a time, adequate evidence before scaling).

## Verified facts

- 30-day free trial (current, resolved by Karen 2026-09-07; `/free-7day-trial/`
  exists in the website repo but is inactive and was not used here)
- $9.99/month or $59.99/year (resolved by Karen 2026-09-07)
- 6,700+ stretch library (resolved by Karen 2026-09-07)
- No equipment required; standing, floor, or mixed positions available
- Continuous animated real-time demonstration (Ada), not a static pose with a
  countdown timer, confirmed real differentiator vs. timer-based competitors
- Routine generated on demand from goals, available time, and history, not
  chosen from a fixed video library

## Audience

- **Primary audience:** Women 50-65, busy and active (still working,
  traveling, gardening, playing golf or pickleball, picking up grandchildren)
- **Awareness levels covered:** problem-aware through ready-to-buy (see each
  angle below)
- **Trigger moments:** socks/shoes, chair rise, car exit, stairs, waking
  stiff, golf, pickleball, travel, gardening, floor time with grandchildren
- **Main objection addressed across the batch:** "I've tried a stretching
  app before and stopped" / "I don't know what to do" / "I don't have time"

## Message

- **Problem:** Small losses of movement have started changing ordinary
  choices, and generic stretching content feels too rigid or disconnected
  from today.
- **Promise:** A routine built in real time around today, not picked from a
  library.
- **Distinct mechanism:** On-demand routine generation from goals, time, and
  history, demonstrated by an avatar that moves through the stretch.
- **Reasons to believe:** Physio-informed stretch library, adjustable duration
  and body focus, progression that steps up on a schedule.
- **Offer:** 30-day free trial.
- **CTA:** Try WeStretch free / Start your free trial.

## Funnel

Meta feed ad -> landing page matching the ad's specific angle (same
audience, problem, promise, and mechanism above the fold) -> free trial
signup -> first routine completed -> paid conversion at day 30.

## Creative directions (the 5 angles)

1. **Everyday Movement Wins**, problem-aware, self-recognition
2. **The Life You're Not Ready to Pause**, desire-led, identity/outcome
3. **A Routine Built for You, Right Now**, solution-aware, mechanism
4. **Better Than What You've Already Tried**, product-aware, objection handling
5. **Try WeStretch Free for 30 Days**, ready-to-buy, offer-led

## Measurement

- **Primary metric:** Cost per trial start
- **Leading metrics:** Outbound CTR, landing-page-view rate, page-to-trial rate
- **Quality guardrails:** Trial-to-first-routine completion (a high-CTR ad
  with weak completion signals curiosity or mismatch, not genuine fit)
- **Decision rule:** Keep the control; iterate an angle with strong attention
  but weak downstream completion; stop an angle after adequate spend with no
  signal; scale only once trial-to-paid and completion both hold up

# Angle 1: Everyday Movement Wins

**Awareness:** Problem-aware. **Job of this angle:** make one exact moment
recognizable enough that the reader thinks "that's me" before they know
anything about the product.

### Ad 1: Socks

- **Hook:** Reaching for socks without the struggle
- **Headline (33 chars):** Reach Your Socks Without Bracing
- **Primary text:** Reaching down for your socks shouldn't take a plan.
  WeStretch builds a short routine around what's tight today, so mornings
  get easier.
- **Visual:** Native, unstaged-feeling photo: a woman sitting on the edge of
  a bed reaching toward a sock, candid indoor morning light, phone or app
  not necessarily visible.
- **Proof:** 6,700+ stretch library; routine built around what's tight today.
- **CTA button:** Sign Up
- **AIDA:** Attention (specific, low-drama photo breaks feed pattern) /
  Interest (names an exact, common moment) / Desire (mornings get easier is
  concrete and picturable) / Action (single clear CTA).
- **Hypothesis:** A specific morning moment (socks) earns a stronger
  self-recognition tap than a generic "stiffness" headline.

### Ad 2: Chair rise

- **Hook:** Standing up from a chair, no hands
- **Headline (34 chars):** Stand Up Without Using Your Hands
- **Primary text:** Notice yourself pushing off the arms of the chair
  lately? A few minutes a day, in a routine built around what's tight right
  now, and standing up stops needing a plan.
- **Visual:** Woman rising from a kitchen chair, natural home lighting,
  phone nearby showing the app mid-routine (subtle, not staged).
- **Proof:** Routine adjusts to today's tight spots, not a fixed video.
- **CTA button:** Sign Up
- **AIDA:** Attention (ordinary home scene, not a fitness ad) / Interest
  (chair rise is a named self-recognition moment) / Desire (implies ease,
  not effort) / Action (single CTA).
- **Hypothesis:** Chair rise is one of the clearest self-recognition moments
  in the research; testing it against socks (Ad 1) for CTR.

### Ad 3: Car exit

- **Hook:** Getting out of the car without the pause
- **Headline (36 chars):** Get Out Of The Car Without The Pause
- **Primary text:** That little pause before you swing your legs out of the
  car. It's worth noticing, and it's not permanent either. WeStretch builds
  today's routine around it.
- **Visual:** Candid photo of a woman getting out of a car in a driveway,
  mid-motion, natural daylight.
- **Proof:** Physio-informed stretch library; routine changes to fit today.
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **AIDA:** Attention (mid-motion moment, unusual for an ad) / Interest
  (near-universal moment for this age group) / Desire ("not permanent"
  reframes without implying failure) / Action (single CTA).
- **Hypothesis:** Marg-style edit from a first draft that read "Without
  Wincing": naming the pain word tells the reader how she feels rather than
  letting her recognize the moment herself. "The pause" is equally specific
  and filmable without the risk.

### Ad 5: Morning stiffness

- **Hook:** Waking up stiff, going into the day anyway

# Angle 3: A Routine Built for You, Right Now

**Awareness:** Solution-aware. **Job of this angle:** name the mechanism
that's different (real-time generation, not a video library) for

- **Primary text:** No scrolling through a video library trying to guess
  which one fits today. WeStretch builds your routine in real time, based on
  what you tell it and what you've already done.
- **Visual:** Screen-recording style: phone showing WeStretch building a
  routine live, Ada demonstrating a movement.
- **Proof:** Routine generated on demand from goals, time, and history, not
  chosen from a fixed list.
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **AIDA:** Attention (product-in-motion visual, distinct from lifestyle
  photos in Angles 1-2) / Interest (speaks to anyone who's browsed a video
  library and given up) / Desire (implies less decision fatigue) / Action
  (single CTA).
- **Hypothesis:** Solution-aware people respond to a named mechanism
  difference; testing whether "not a menu" outperforms a pure-benefit
  headline.

### Ad 12: Time input

- **Hook:** Tell it how much time you have
- **Headline (33 chars):** Give It 10 Minutes, Get A Routine
- **Primary text:** Some days there's twenty minutes. Some days there's
  five. Tell WeStretch what you've got and it builds a routine that fits,
  instead of a fixed class you have to make time for.
- **Visual:** Simple UI capture showing a time selector, then the routine
  building, paired with a real photo of a woman starting the routine.
- **Proof:** Adjustable duration input drives the generated routine.
- **CTA button:** Sign Up
- **AIDA:** Attention (UI-forward, feels like a demo, interrupts lifestyle

  Desire (shows the fit instead of only claiming it) / Action (single CTA).

### Ad 13: Body Filter

- **Headline (37 chars):** Skip What's Sore, Focus What Needs It
- **Primary text:** If your lower back is having a day, tell WeStretch and

- **Proof:** Continuous animated demonstration vs. a static pose with a

- **AIDA:** Attention (comparison format stands out) / Interest (speaks
  directly to anyone who's used a timer-based app) / Desire (removes
  guesswork, a concrete frustration) / Action (single CTA).
- **Hypothesis:** This is WeStretch's clearest true differentiator against
  timer-based competitors. Testing a direct comparison ad for CTR among
  people who've tried other stretching apps.

### Ad 15: Progression
