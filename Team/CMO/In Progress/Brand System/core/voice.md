# Brand Voice, short reference

Full principles live in `Team/CXO/In Progress/Onboarding UX Flow Spec/Brand-Voice-Principles.md`.
This file only carries the rules that constrain design output.

## Hard rules, no exceptions

1. **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Company wide. Use a period, comma, colon, semicolon or brackets.
2. **Never say the app "learns", "watches" or "gets to know" the user's body.**
   The real mechanism is physiotherapist recommended progressions plus a scheduled
   step up in hold time and difficulty. Use "physio backed picks" or
   "your holds step up on schedule".
3. **Drop optional commas** around inserted names. "Nice work {first_name}" not "Nice work, {first_name}".
4. **Copy that looks misspelled gets confirmed with Karen before it is rendered.** Never silently corrected, never shipped as is.

## Audience

Adults 50 to 65 who want to stay active, mobile and independent.
The promise is continuing to do what matters, not fitness for its own sake.

## Characters

- **Ada**: main AI instructor. Motivator plus explainer. Smart, adaptive, calm.
- **Bruce**: secondary coach. High energy, celebratory.

Their in app lines are in `core/brand-core.json`. Match that register.
