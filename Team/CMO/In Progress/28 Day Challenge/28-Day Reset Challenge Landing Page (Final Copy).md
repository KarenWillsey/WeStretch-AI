### Questions before you join?

**What if I'm really stiff or haven't stretched in a long time?**

That's completely fine. You don't need to be flexible or already have a stretching habit to join. The challenge is simply about spending a little time each day helping your body feel and move better.

**Is this actually safe for me?**

Every stretch was developed and validated with physiotherapists and movement specialists, who mapped which stretches are safe and in what order. WeStretch uses what you tell it about what's bothering you to decide which of those stretches you get. If something's off-limits for you, it's left out.

**Do I have to do all 15 minutes at once?**

No. You can spread your stretching throughout the day. Do a few stretches when you have time and finish the rest later. What matters is making those 15 minutes part of your day.

**Do I have to do all 28 days?**

The goal is to give your body 15 minutes a day for 28 days and see what changes. But if you miss a day, just get back to it the next day. The point is consistency, not perfection.

**What if a different area starts bothering me?**

That's okay. Your stretching is personalized, so it can change with you. If your neck is bothering you today and your hips feel tight tomorrow, WeStretch can adjust what you're working on.

**Are there prizes?**

Yes. You'll have a chance to win prizes each week for participating, plus the chance to win one of two grand prizes at the end of the challenge.

**When does the challenge start?**

The 28-Day Reset starts October 19 and runs through November 15. Everyone does the challenge over the same four weeks.

**How do I join?**

Join WeStretch with a monthly or annual subscription. You'll get full access to the app right away, so you can start stretching immediately. Then on October 19, you'll get access to the 28-Day Reset Challenge.
