| Ratio | Filename ending | Size |
|---|---|---|

| Portrait feed | `1080x1350.png` | 1080 x 1350 |

Example: `reset-r01-v01-1080x1350.png` is R01's portrait feed image.

## Before the Meta expert launches

- Confirm the live Reset destination, subscription/trial terms, campaign year
  and dates. The artwork intentionally contains no prices or start dates.
- Confirm budget, target acquisition cost, audience and conversion event.
- Preview every active placement in Ads Manager. Text, logo and CTA in the
  vertical artwork stay inside x 90 to 990, y 280 to 1134. Actual Meta
  interface previews have not been checked in this session.
- Review automatic enhancements so approved copy, crops and people stay intact.
- Verify checkout and conversion measurement, including deduplication where
  browser and server events are both sent.
- Use neutral creative IDs such as `utm_content=r01_v01`. Keep shared copy,
  destination and offer consistent in the first three-concept test.

Judge by the verified acquisition event and conversion quality. Use click
metrics diagnostically. The test budget and launch settings were not supplied,
so no campaign setup or launch was performed.
