Same identity anchor as Flow 2 and Flow 3: "I'm someone who takes care of how I move," rising Six-Axis readiness from day 7 (openness only) to day 30 (identity/compliance).

**Guardrail flag applied proactively:** the Flow 2 review surfaced a real failure mode, long-horizon emails (day 23, day 30) sliding into decline/stakes framing ("if you don't keep this up..."). Day 23's "adapts" and Day 30's "protects" are the two Flow 1 themes most exposed to that same trap ("adapts" invites a versus-atrophy comparison, "protects" invites a versus-loss framing). Both are written gain-only below: what movement keeps open, never what's lost without it. Neither needed a second loop because the lesson was applied during drafting rather than caught after.

## Final copy: FLOW 1

## Day 7

**Subject:** Feeling good doesn't happen by accident
**Preview:** It's built, five minutes at a time.

Hi {{first_name | default:"there"}},

It's been about a week since your last stretch.

Feeling good in your body isn't something that happens to some people and not others. It's built, in small ordinary moments like this one, and kept up the same way.

Today's five minutes is one of those moments. Not a big restart, just the next one.

[Start today's routine]

The WeStretch Team

## Day 11

**Subject:** There's nothing to catch up on
**Preview:** Every day starts clean.

Hi {{first_name | default:"there"}},

It's easy to assume a week away means a week to make up for.

It doesn't work that way here. Every day starts clean. The days you missed don't sit there waiting to be paid back, they're just gone. There's only today, and whatever you do with the next five minutes.

Nothing to catch up on. Just somewhere to start.

[Start today's routine]

The WeStretch Team

## Day 16

**Subject:** You don't need to feel like it
**Preview:** Motivation shows up after, not before.

Hi {{first_name | default:"there"}},

Here's a small, useful secret: you don't have to feel like stretching to start stretching.

Motivation is funny that way, it tends to show up after the first minute, not before it. So skip waiting for the mood and go straight to the five minutes. The feeling of wanting to keeps up on its own.

Start now. The motivation will catch up.

[Start today's routine]

The WeStretch Team

## Day 23

**Subject:** Your body is quietly paying attention
**Preview:** It adapts to whatever you give it.

Hi {{first_name | default:"there"}},

Your body doesn't need grand gestures to change. It adapts to whatever you consistently give it.

A few minutes of movement, done regularly, and things loosen that used to catch. Reaching gets a little easier. Getting up off the floor stops being an event.

Give it a little time and notice for yourself.

[Start today's routine]

The WeStretch Team

## Day 30

**Subject:** This isn't really about stretching
**Preview:** It's about everything stretching lets you keep doing.

Hi {{first_name | default:"there"}},

Five minutes of movement was never really the point. It's what that five minutes keeps open: the walk you take without thinking about it, the floor you sit on and get back up from, the reach to the top shelf that just works.

That's the actual prize. Stretching is just how you keep the door open.

Today's five minutes is a small, ordinary way of keeping it that way.

[Start today's routine]

The WeStretch Team
