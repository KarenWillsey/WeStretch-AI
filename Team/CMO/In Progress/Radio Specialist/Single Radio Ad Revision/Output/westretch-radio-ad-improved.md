# WeStretch Radio Ad — Improved for Read-Aloud Delivery

## Latest version (83 words, ~30 sec at a natural announcer pace)

Golf, yard work, camping weekends, and time with family. There's a lot in life you want to keep enjoying, so don't let pain and stiffness get in the way.

WeStretch is an easy stretching app, designed to keep you ready for what matters most.

Just press start and follow simple, guided routines personalized for your body, your time, and your goals.

No guesswork. No gym. Stretching at your fingertips.

Download WeStretch today at WeStretch dot CA, and keep doing the things you love.

---

## What changed this round and why

Kari (Karen's personal assistant) reviewed the previous version and said it didn't read like a natural conversation. She supplied a rewrite, which Karen adopted:

- **Opening restructured into two shorter, plainer sentences** instead of one sentence with a dash-linked clause: "Golf, yard work, camping weekends, and time with family. There's a lot in life you want to keep enjoying, so don't let pain and stiffness get in the way." — reads more like speech, less like written copy.
- **"An easy stretching solution on your phone" → "an easy stretching app"** — drops "solution" (corporate/written word) and "on your phone" (redundant — it's implicit for an app). This independently confirms an earlier note that "solution" doesn't read naturally out loud.
- **Every em dash removed, including from the CTA close** ("CA—and" → "CA, and") — per a new company-wide rule from Karen at the Manager level: no em dashes anywhere in any company output. See root `Memory.md`.
- Net length is essentially unchanged (84 → 83 words), so no further trimming was needed — it still fits comfortably in a natural 30-second read.

Full guidance logged in this project's `Memory.md` (natural-phrasing feedback) and the repo root `Memory.md` (the em dash rule, which applies company-wide, not just to this project).
