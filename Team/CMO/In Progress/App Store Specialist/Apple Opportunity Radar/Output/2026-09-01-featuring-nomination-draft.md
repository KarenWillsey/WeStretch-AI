## Nomination pitch copy

**What are you nominating?** In-App Event: "7-Day Mobility Challenge"

**One-line pitch:**
"A 7-day guided mobility challenge that adapts to each person's own body,
not a fixed template everyone gets the same way."

**Why WeStretch deserves featuring** (mapped directly to Apple's stated
evaluation criteria — user experience, UI design, innovation, uniqueness
vs. competitors, accessibility, localization quality, and product page
quality):

- **Innovation / uniqueness:** Most stretch and mobility apps hand every
  user the same fixed program. WeStretch's stretch selection is
  personalized to the individual's own body and movement history every
  day, so the 7-day challenge has a shared theme (Day 1–2 baseline
  mobility, Day 3–4 targeted stiffness relief, Day 5–6 range of motion,
  Day 7 wrap-up) but a different actual routine per user. The Challenge
  format itself is new to WeStretch's App Store presence — this would be
  its first.
- **User experience / UI design:** Sessions are guided by Ada, WeStretch's
  animated character, with continuous movement demonstration rather than a
  static image and a countdown timer.
- **Physiotherapist-informed foundation:** The movement library and safe
  movement framework are mapped by licensed physiotherapists; the app's
  personalization layer adapts that general framework to each user, not
  the other way around (attribution kept precise per brand guardrail —
  physiotherapists map the general framework, the app personalizes it).
- **Accessibility and older-adult usability:** WeStretch is positioned and
  designed around being usable by older adults specifically (larger tap
  targets, clear step-by-step guidance, no reliance on fast timed reps).
  Note: formal accessibility compliance (Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Nutrition
  Labels, etc.) is tracked as a separate, still-open checklist — see
  `Output/2026-08-31-accessibility-privacy-subscription-clarity-checklist.md`.
  Don't cite specific accessibility features as confirmed in the actual
  nomination until that checklist is signed off; this draft speaks to
  design intent/positioning only, not compliance claims.
- **Localization quality:** Screenshot and app-preview-video copy is
  localized into French and Spanish (see
  `Output/2026-08-26-localize-app-store-assets-fr-es.md`). Note: full
  product page metadata (name/subtitle/keywords/description) is
  English-only today — that's a separate, still-open backlog item. Don't
  overstate localization in the actual nomination beyond what's true at
  submission time; check that item's status before submitting.
- **Product page quality:** First three screenshots have an optimized
  treatment with a Product Page Optimization test drafted (see
  `Output/2026-08-22-ppo-test-first-3-screenshots.md`), and five new
  Custom Product Pages targeting specific use cases (back pain, stiffness,
  mobility, golf, pickleball) are planned (see
  `Output/2026-08-23-cpp-plan-5-pages.md`).

**Suggested supporting context to include in the nomination form's "tell
us more" field:**
"WeStretch pairs a physiotherapist-informed movement library with

Challenge' get an identical routine, even though they share the same

underserved by fixed-template fitness apps, including older adults and
