# Get reviewer permission before quoting any App Store review in marketing materials

Backlog item addressed: "Get reviewer permission before quoting any App Store review in marketing materials; Apple requires explicit reviewer consent for this."

## The rule

Quoting a customer's App Store review text in any marketing material
(website, ads, social, App Store product page, App Store Events, decks,
etc.) requires that reviewer's explicit permission first. A star rating
can be used in marketing without per-reviewer permission, but only if it
accurately reflects the app's *current* overall rating, not a
cherry-picked historical high.

## Process: from candidate quote to cleared-for-use

1. **Source candidate quotes.** Whoever monitors App Store reviews
   (App Store Connect → Ratings and Reviews) flags reviews that are
   strong, specific, and on-brand, e.g. mentions of pain relief,
   independence, ease of use, older-adult accessibility. Keep a running
   list (see "Where to track this" below) rather than acting ad hoc.
2. **Request consent via Apple's review-response mechanism.** App Store
   Connect lets developers reply publicly to a review; the reviewer is
   notified of the reply. Use that reply to ask permission to feature
   their words in marketing, and ask them to confirm via a reply of their
   own (public reviewer replies are visible, so this doubles as a
   timestamped consent record).
   - Do not use a private/backend contact method to solicit consent if
     one isn't already established; Apple's rating/review guidelines are
     about not incentivizing or backchanneling reviews, so keep the ask
     itself confined to the public reply thread.
   - Never offer a reward, discount, or incentive in exchange for
     permission; that's a separate, harder Apple prohibition (no
     rewards in exchange for reviews) and would taint the consent itself.
3. **Record the consent.** Once a reviewer confirms (reply, or any other
   channel Apple later provides), capture: reviewer's displayed name/
   handle, the exact review text being quoted, the date consent was
   given, and where/how they confirmed. Store this next to wherever the
   quote gets used (see "Where to track this").
4. **Quote only what was consented to.** If a reviewer approves quoting
   their review, use their words as given; don't edit/trim in a way that
   changes meaning without re-confirming, and don't reuse a quote in a
   new context (e.g. moving from website testimonial to a paid ad) without
   checking the original consent's scope, if the reviewer limited it.
5. **Re-check star-rating claims separately.** If marketing cites a star
   rating (not a quote), confirm it matches the *current* live App Store
   rating before each use; this doesn't need reviewer consent, but it
   does need a freshness check every time it's reused, since the live
   rating moves.

No tracking file exists yet for consented review quotes. Recommend a

Store Description Copywriting/reviewer-consent-log.md` or similar, once
CMO has an active project folder that actually uses testimonial quotes,

channel, where it's been used. Not created here since no marketing asset
is currently using a review quote yet; create it at the point a first
quote is actually cleared for use, so it doesn't sit empty.

## What "pass" looks like

No review quote appears anywhere in WeStretch marketing without a
recorded, reviewer-confirmed consent behind it. Star-rating claims are
re-verified against the live current rating at time of use, every time.
