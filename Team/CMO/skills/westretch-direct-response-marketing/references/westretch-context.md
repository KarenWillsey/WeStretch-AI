# WeStretch context

Use this reference for WeStretch-facing strategy and copy. Verify volatile commercial details before publication.

## Product

WeStretch is a mobile and web stretching and mobility app from We Bananas Software Inc. It creates a fully demonstrated routine in real time rather than asking the user to choose a fixed prerecorded class.

The routine can adapt to inputs such as:

- Available time
- Goal or routine style
- Body areas to focus on or avoid
- Standing, floor, or mixed positions
- Speed, coach, environment, audio, and captions
- Current stage or ability

The app uses animated coaches, including Ada and Bruce. It supports English, French, and Spanish. Its large stretch library and progression system allow routine variety rather than the same fixed sequence each time.

## Positioning

### WHO

A person who will act now to relieve pain and stiffness while restoring or expanding mobility.

### Essence

**Able**

### WHAT

WeStretch instantly creates a fully demonstrated, on-demand mobility routine that is personalized and unique every time.

### Desired emotional outcome

The customer should think: "This could be me. I can continue enjoying the things I love."

## Primary acquisition audience

The broad audience is adults approximately 45-65. Women approximately 50-65 are often the strongest primary audience for acquisition creative. Secondary audiences include golf, pickleball, desk work, and physically demanding work.

Do not treat these ranges as product restrictions. A campaign may select a narrower audience to improve message relevance.

## Core customer tensions

- The customer feels younger than their body sometimes behaves.
- Small losses of movement have started changing ordinary choices.
- They know they should stretch but do not know what to do, for how long, or which movements fit today.
- Generic videos feel too rigid, repetitive, difficult, or disconnected from today's needs.
- Classes require travel, schedules, equipment, or confidence.
- They want relief and continued independence without turning stretching into a major project.

## Useful self-recognition moments

- Struggling to reach socks or shoes
- Feeling stiff after a flight or long drive
- Losing comfort or range in a golf or pickleball movement
- Using the hands to rise from a chair
- Pausing or wobbling when exiting a car
- Avoiding stairs
- Bracing before a normal movement
- Waking with stiffness
- Getting up from the floor with difficulty
- Feeling locked up after sitting

Use these as hypotheses until supported by customer evidence for the specific campaign.

## Differentiators to translate into benefits

| Product capability | Customer meaning |
|---|---|
| Routine generated on demand | No searching through a video library or guessing what to do |
| Large stretch library | Greater variety and a better fit for different goals and body needs |
| Adjustable duration | Fits the time available today |
| Body Filter | Focus on one area or avoid another |
| Pose and position controls | Choose standing, floor, or mixed movement based on comfort and setting |
| Progression | The experience can develop instead of remaining fixed |
| Multiple routine styles | One app can support mobility, pain relief, sport, strength, aerobic work, and other goals |
| Animated real-time demonstration | Makes unique routines possible without requiring a prerecorded class for every combination |

## Offer separation

### Regular WeStretch Pro

The normal subscription unlocks broader personalization, routine styles, unlimited use, and additional app features. It may include a free trial. Verify the current trial length, prices, and exact entitlements.

### 28-Day Reset

The 28-Day Reset is a separate paid challenge. Do not present it as part of the regular subscription funnel unless the user explicitly requests that relationship.
