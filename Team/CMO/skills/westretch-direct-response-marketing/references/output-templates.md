Use these structures as working schemas. Adapt their size to the request.

## Halo research table

```markdown
| ID | Source | Exact language | Category | Frequency | Intensity | Relevance | Evidence strength | Possible use |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
```

After collection, produce:

```markdown
## Ranked findings
- Top 10 pains
- Top 10 desires
- Top 10 objections
- Top 10 trigger moments
- Top 5 failed alternatives
- Top 5 reasons to believe
- Top 10 exact customer phrases
```

## Static-ad matrix

```markdown
| ID | Angle | Awareness | Audience moment | Hook | Visual | Primary text | Headline | Proof | CTA | Hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

For each ad, run the AIDA check:

```markdown
- Attention: Pass/Fail - why
- Interest: Pass/Fail - why
- Desire: Pass/Fail - why
- Action: Pass/Fail - why
```

```markdown
[Large benefit]

[How and why the benefit is plausible]

## Intrigue
- [Six strongest bullets]

[Customer-language problem section]

## The solution
[Distinct mechanism]

## Why believe us
[Credentials and proof]

## What this means for you
[Benefits]

## Customer proof
[Testimonials/reviews with permission]

## Offer
[Exactly what is included]

## Bonuses
[Only genuine deliverables]

## Value
[Factual value explanation]

## Price and terms
[VERIFY and state clearly]

## Genuine urgency
[Omit if none]

## Risk reversal
[Trial, cancellation, supported guarantee]

## CTA
[One direct command]

## P.S.
[Outcome + offer + risk reversal + consequence + CTA]
```
