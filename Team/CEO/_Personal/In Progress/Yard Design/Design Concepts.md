# Yard Design Concepts

## Recommendation

Start with **Concept 1: Continuous Frame**. It repeats the current yard's
perimeter framing, leaves the centre open and uses the fewest plants needed to
make the three properties read as one landscape.

## Non-negotiable layout rules

- Every hill and plant stays inside the green parcel boundary.
- A continuous planted hill frames the road, outside and rear edges.
- The frame connects visually to the existing perimeter planting.
- The centre of each parcel stays open for play.
- Grass connects the new lawns to the current lawn.
- Curves and passages remain broad enough for a robot mower.

## Concept 1: Continuous Frame

- A light U-shaped hill frames each parcel.
- Three trees per parcel.
- Maximum uninterrupted play lawn.
- Best balance of continuity, simplicity and mowing.

## Concept 2: Rolling Frame

- A wider perimeter hill rises and falls along the three outside edges.
- Four trees per parcel.
- Closest match to the dimensional hills in the current yard.
- The centre remains one clear play field.

## Concept 3: Tree-Anchor Frame

- A narrow continuous perimeter hill.
- Three trees per parcel, concentrated at outside corners.
- Most open grass and least planting.
- Strong structure with the easiest future removal.

## Before choosing plants

- Measure both parcels and mark property lines.
- Locate utilities and drainage.
- Sketch likely future house, driveway and construction access areas.
- Confirm irrigation reach and robot mower boundary requirements.
- Have a local landscape professional confirm grading, drainage and species.
