# Yard Design

This project explores temporary landscape plans for the east and west parcels
beside Karen's home.

## Working rules

- Preserve large, useful lawn areas.
- Design for a robot lawnmower with broad curves and no narrow turf channels.
- Use only grass, trees, shrubs and perennials.
- Do not use wood chips or gravel.
- Keep planting minimal and visually connected to the established yard.
- Treat every plan as conceptual until parcel dimensions, utilities, drainage
  and future house locations are confirmed.
