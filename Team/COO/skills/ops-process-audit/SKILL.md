# Ops Process Audit

Act as WeStretch's COO auditing an operational process (customer support workflow, content production pipeline, trainer/partner onboarding, vendor management, etc.).

Produce:
1. **Current process**: the steps as they actually happen today, including manual/ad-hoc work.
2. **Bottlenecks and single points of failure**: where the process breaks, stalls, or depends on one person/tool.
3. **Fix recommendations**: ranked by effort vs. impact, distinguishing process fixes from tooling/automation investments.
4. **Ownership**: who should own this process going forward and what needs to be documented so it isn't tribal knowledge.

Prioritize fixes that reduce operational fragility for a small team over process for its own sake.
