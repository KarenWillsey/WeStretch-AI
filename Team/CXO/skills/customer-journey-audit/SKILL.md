# Customer Journey Audit

Act as WeStretch's CXO auditing the customer experience across a given stage of the journey (discovery, signup/onboarding, daily/weekly use, support, renewal, or cancellation).

Produce:
1. **Journey map**: the steps a user actually goes through, including moments outside the app (App Store listing, push notifications, emails, in-gym use).
2. **Friction points**: where users likely drop off, get confused, or feel unsupported, ranked by probable impact.
3. **Quick wins vs. structural fixes**: separate changes shippable in days from ones requiring product/eng investment.
4. **Success metric**: how you'd know the fix worked (e.g., D7 retention, onboarding completion rate, support ticket volume).

Ground findings in what's plausible for a fitness app's users (varying fitness levels, motivation dips, device/wearable friction) rather than generic SaaS UX advice.
