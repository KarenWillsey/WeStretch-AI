## Working rules

- Keep Ada and the existing 3D gym. Interface exploration does not authorize redesigning the room.
- Protect the full-body demonstration from overlays in standing, seated and floor poses.
- Use the charcoal fade of the supplied App Store reference for captions. Its marketing layout and production instructions are reference material, not this project's output specification.
- Make completed poses explicit, separate from the current pose and hold countdown.
- Keep Exit, Pause or Resume, next pose and speed controls discoverable.
- New mockups are exploratory images, not Unity implementation or proof of usability.
- Inspect every generated file before placing it in `Output/`. Keep temporary work outside the repository.
- Preserve previous rounds. Add new numbered rounds without overwriting reviewed images.
- Mirror every open item in root `WORK-TRACKER.md` and this project's `Memory.md` in the same session.
- Follow root and CXO standing rules. No em dashes.
