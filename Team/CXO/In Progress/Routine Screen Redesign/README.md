## What is included

- Nine portrait images and one landscape option.
- Charcoal gradient captions based on the supplied App Store reference.
- Hold countdowns positioned clear of the active Ada in the pictured pose.
- Completed-pose counts with visual routine progress.
- Exit, Pause or Resume, next pose and model-speed controls.
- Normal, slowed, slowest and paused examples across the set.

## Review notes

- Start by comparing 01, 02 and 05 for a clear portrait hierarchy. Compare 04 for a large side timer and 10 for landscape. This is the CXO's first visual shortlist, not a tested winner.
- These are AI-generated design concepts, not production screens. Small scenery, character and lettering details vary from the source screenshots. Those variations are not proposed gym changes. Implementation must use the unchanged existing 3D gym and Ada assets.
- Font sizes, tap targets, contrast and clearance through every pose need verification in a real interface. Understanding after two stretches has not been tested.
- Sample state is 12 / 66 completed, about 18% progress, and 18 hold seconds. Speed values are illustrative.
- Each running design uses Pause; that same control becomes Resume when paused. Concept 09 explicitly illustrates Resume. Normal-speed designs reveal Slower and Faster after the first slowdown.
- Images keep their original generated dimensions. They are not App Store exports or final native UI specifications.
