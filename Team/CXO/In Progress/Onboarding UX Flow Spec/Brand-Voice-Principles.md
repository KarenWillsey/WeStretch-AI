## History

Karen dictated five principles on 2026-08-20 as standing rules for every screen
and sheet in this spec. They were merged into the main voice document on
2026-09-09. Here is what happened to each.

| Original principle | Where it lives now |
|---|---|
| 1. The app is intelligent and personalized. Every routine is built from the user's history and today's customizations. | **"Evolves with you"** in `core/voice.md`. Kept, but the explanation was corrected: the app runs a physiotherapist-decided progression on schedule. It does not learn or observe the user's body. |
| 2. Ada is smart and advancing. Hold times and difficulty increase as the user improves. Copy should credit Ada with tracking and advancing the user. | **"Ada and Bruce"** and **"Evolves with you"**. Kept, with one change: the word **tracking is retired**. It carries the same observational implication as "learning" and is now on the avoid list. "Advancing" stays. |
| 3. The core goal is to inspire daily stretching by motivating users to come back. | Carried through the whole document, and specifically the tone ladder and the Product copy channel section. |
| 4. Conversion lever: FOMO on invested progress. | **"Progress is an asset, not a debt"**. Kept as a deliberate lever, reframed from what the reader loses to what the reader keeps. Karen approved this scoping on 2026-09-09. |
| 5. Speak to the user's ego and their point of view. Copy is about what they've achieved, their body, their progress. | Carried through. Confirmed 2026-09-09 to mean their achievement, not flattery. |

Nothing was dropped. All five survive in the merged document.
