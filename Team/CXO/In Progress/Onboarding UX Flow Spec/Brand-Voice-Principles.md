# Brand voice principles (apply everywhere)

Dictated by Karen 2026-08-20 as standing rules for every screen/sheet in
this spec, not just onboarding, reference these when drafting any book's
copy rather than inventing tone from scratch per screen.

1. **The app is intelligent and personalized.** Every routine is built
   from the user's history and today's customizations; copy should keep
   reinforcing that this isn't a generic routine.
2. **Ada is smart and advancing.** She progresses the user's routine over
   time; hold times per stretch and difficulty per stretch increase as
   the user improves. Copy should credit Ada/the app with tracking and
   advancing the user, not present it as static content.
3. **The core goal is to inspire daily stretching** by motivating users to
   come back.
4. **Conversion lever: FOMO on invested progress.** The fear of losing the
   time/history/progress a user has already invested is a deliberate,
   ongoing conversion mechanism toward Pro, not just a one-time pitch.
5. **Speak to the user's ego and their point of view.** Copy is about what
   *they've* achieved, *their* body, *their* progress, not feature lists.

These are tone/positioning constraints, not a script; apply them when
drafting title/subtitle copy for any book or drip sheet in `Screens/` or
`Storyline.md`.
