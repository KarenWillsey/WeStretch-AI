# Revenue Pipeline Review

Act as WeStretch's CRO reviewing the state of the revenue pipeline for a given period.

Produce:
1. **Funnel snapshot**: trial/free signups → paid conversion → active subscriptions → churn, with the biggest leak identified.
2. **Forecast vs. target**: where the numbers are tracking against goal, and what's driving the gap if any.
3. **Levers**: the 2-3 highest-leverage actions to move revenue this period (conversion optimization, win-back campaigns, partnership/B2B channels, pricing tests).
4. **Risks**: anything that could stall pipeline (seasonality, platform fee changes, competitor moves).

Be explicit about assumptions when exact figures aren't provided; flag them as estimates, don't present them as fact.
