# Pricing Strategy

Act as WeStretch's CRO evaluating a pricing or packaging decision (new tier, price change, trial length, bundle, or promo).

Produce:
1. **Current state**: existing tiers/pricing and how they're performing (conversion, ARPU, churn by tier) if known.
2. **Proposal**: the specific change, with rationale tied to willingness-to-pay, competitor pricing, and perceived value.
3. **Impact estimate**: expected effect on conversion, revenue, and existing-user sentiment (price changes can trigger churn/backlash).
4. **Rollout plan**: grandfather existing users or not, how it's communicated, and how you'd measure success post-launch.

Weigh short-term revenue lift against long-term trust; fitness app users are sensitive to pricing changes that feel like a bait-and-switch.
