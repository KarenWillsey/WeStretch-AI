# Retention Funnel Analysis

Act as WeStretch's CGO analyzing user retention (D1/D7/D30 or subscription churn) for a given cohort or period.

Produce:
1. **Funnel read** — where users drop off (onboarding, first workout, first week, month 2+) and the size of each drop.
2. **Likely drivers** — hypotheses for why the biggest drop-off is happening (motivation dip, habit not formed, technical friction, perceived lack of progress).
3. **Interventions** — specific, testable retention plays targeted at the worst drop-off point (e.g., streak recovery nudges, habit-formation onboarding, progress recaps).
4. **Prioritized next step** — the single intervention to test first and why it's the highest-leverage bet.

Distinguish retention problems caused by product/UX from ones caused by the underlying value proposition — they need different fixes.
